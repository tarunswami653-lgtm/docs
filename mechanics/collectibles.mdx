Two types of items appear on top of the wooden platform segments in Turbo: collectibles that boost your score and hazards that set you back. Before you even land on a plank, the **glow beneath each item** tells you exactly what you are dealing with — green means a reward is waiting, red means danger is ahead. Reading these visual cues quickly and adjusting your path on the fly is one of the most important skills in the game.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Turbo Chips" icon="bag-shopping">
    **Type:** Reward / Collectible

    Turbo Chips appear as a **green bag** resting on top of a platform segment. A soft **green glow** radiates from beneath the plank directly under the bag, making them easy to spot from a distance.

    **Effect on collection:**
    - Awards points toward your score immediately on contact.
    - Contributes to combo chains if collected in quick succession.
    - The platform segment becomes empty after pickup — safe to re-land on.

    **Spawn behavior:**
    Turbo Chips spawn on both LEFT and RIGHT segments across the top, upper-middle, and lower-middle rows. The bottom row never holds Turbo Chips. Item density and placement pattern change with each level, so no two stages have an identical chip layout.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Turbo Soda" icon="can-food">
    **Type:** Hazard / Penalty Item

    Turbo Soda appears as a **red can** sitting on top of a platform segment. A bright **red glow** shines from beneath the plank, serving as a clear warning signal that the segment should be avoided.

    **Effect on contact:**
    - Triggers a penalty that deducts points from your current score.
    - Breaks any active combo chain, resetting your multiplier to 1×.
    - The can disappears after contact — the segment is safe on subsequent landings.

    **Spawn behavior:**
    Turbo Soda cans spawn on both LEFT and RIGHT segments of active rows alongside Turbo Chips. They are never placed on the bottom row. The ratio of chips to soda cans varies by level difficulty — harder levels place more cans in high-traffic positions, forcing riskier navigation decisions.
  </Card>

## Glow Color System

The glow beneath each item is your primary navigation guide in Turbo. Because items can appear anywhere on any active platform segment, training yourself to scan glow colors before committing to a landing saves you from avoidable penalties.

| Glow Color | Item | Action |
|------------|------|--------|
| 🟢 Green | Turbo Chips | Land on this segment to collect and score |
| 🔴 Red | Turbo Soda | Avoid this segment to prevent a penalty |
| *(none)* | Empty segment | Safe to land — no effect |

## Item Spawning and Density

Items are distributed across platform rows at the start of each level. As you collect Turbo Chips and accidentally (or deliberately) trigger Turbo Soda cans, those segments become empty. The level does not respawn items mid-run — once a segment is cleared, it stays empty for the remainder of that attempt. Planning an efficient collection route that avoids cans while sweeping up chips before falling through gaps is a core part of Turbo's strategic depth.
