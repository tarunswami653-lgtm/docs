Your score in Turbo reflects every decision you make on the platform grid. Collecting Turbo Chips earns you points, touching Turbo Soda cans costs you points, and chaining collections together without interruption multiplies everything. Whether you are aiming to clear a level cleanly or chasing a personal best, understanding how each action translates into a number on the screen gives you a concrete edge over a purely reactive playstyle.

## How Points Are Awarded

Every Turbo Chips bag you collect adds points to your running total. The base value of each chip collection is fixed, but that value scales with your active combo multiplier. Reaching items on higher platform tiers — where Turbo Soda cans are more densely placed — does not award bonus points on its own, but the increased risk of breaking your combo makes careful navigation on those tiers more rewarding when you pull it off cleanly.

## Scoring Table

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Collect Turbo Chips (base, no combo) | +100 pts |
| Collect Turbo Chips at 2× combo | +200 pts |
| Collect Turbo Chips at 3× combo | +300 pts |
| Collect Turbo Chips at 4× combo | +400 pts |
| Touch Turbo Soda (penalty) | −150 pts |
| Touch Turbo Soda (breaks combo) | Multiplier resets to 1× |
| Complete level with no soda contact | +500 pts bonus |

## Combo Multipliers

Your combo multiplier increases by 1× each time you collect a Turbo Chips bag without touching a Turbo Soda can in between. The multiplier caps at **4×** and persists until you either trigger a soda can or your current run ends.

- **1× (default):** Active from the start of every run and after any soda contact.
- **2×:** Reached after two consecutive chip collections with no interruption.
- **3×:** Reached after four consecutive chip collections with no interruption.
- **4× (max):** Reached after six consecutive chip collections — every chip collected at this tier is worth four times the base value.

Because touching Turbo Soda resets your multiplier all the way back to 1×, a single careless landing on a red-glowing segment can erase a significant scoring advantage built up over careful play.

## Penalties

Turbo Soda cans penalize you in two ways simultaneously: they subtract a flat **150 points** from your total and reset your combo multiplier. If your score drops below zero, it is clamped at zero rather than going negative — but losing a high combo multiplier at the wrong moment is often more damaging to your final score than the point deduction itself.

## High Score Tracking

Turbo records your **all-time highest score** separately from your current run total. After each run ends — whether from falling through the bottom gap or completing all collectibles — your score is compared against your saved high score. If you beat it, the new score is saved immediately. High scores persist across sessions, giving you a long-term benchmark to measure improvement.

<Tip>
  To maximize your score, prioritize building your combo multiplier before sweeping higher tiers. Start on the lower-middle row where item density is more manageable, string together several clean chip collections to reach 3× or 4×, then move up to the top rows where chips are worth their full multiplied value. Avoiding even a single Turbo Soda can throughout the entire run also unlocks the **+500 pts level completion bonus**, which can be the difference between a good score and a great one.
</Tip>
