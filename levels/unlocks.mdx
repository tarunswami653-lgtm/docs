Progression in Turbo is driven by a combination of score accumulation, item clearing, and milestone thresholds that gate access to new level screens, item types, and rewards. As you push further into the game, the challenges you face grow more complex — and the rewards for overcoming them become more substantial. This page walks you through everything you need to know about how unlocks and progression milestones work.

## How Level Unlocks Work

New level screens are unlocked by meeting one or both of the following conditions:

- **Clearing the required items** on the current screen — collecting or neutralizing all active Turbo Soda and Turbo Chips (and any higher-tier items) triggers a screen transition.
- **Reaching a score threshold** — some milestones are gated by your cumulative score rather than (or in addition to) item clearing. If your score hasn't reached the required value, the next screen won't unlock even if the current one is cleared.

Once a new screen unlocks, you are transported to it automatically. Your current score carries over, and any streak or combo multipliers remain active.

## Score Thresholds for Progression

Score thresholds increase with each level you complete. Early screens have forgiving minimums, while later screens demand consistent item collection and combo maintenance to advance.

| Level Tier | Score Threshold to Unlock Next | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Levels 1–3) | 500 pts per level | Introductory — all unlocks are item-clear based |
| Tier 2 (Levels 4–6) | 1,500 pts cumulative | Score gate activates; combos become important |
| Tier 3 (Levels 7–10) | 4,000 pts cumulative | New item types begin appearing |
| Tier 4 (Levels 11–15) | 10,000 pts cumulative | High-difficulty spawns on previously blank rows |
| Tier 5 (Levels 16+) | 25,000 pts cumulative | Maximum difficulty scaling; all rows can be occupied |

## Rewards for Completing Levels

Completing a level screen rewards you with a combination of bonus points, cosmetic unlocks, and gameplay modifiers. The type of reward depends on how cleanly you completed the screen.

- **Perfect Clear Bonus** — awarded when you clear all items on a screen without falling through the center gap. Grants a significant score multiplier for the next screen.
- **Speed Bonus** — awarded when you clear a screen faster than the par time. The faster you clear, the larger the bonus.
- **Streak Reward** — awarded for completing multiple screens in a row without taking damage or falling. Streak rewards compound with each consecutive clean screen.
- **Cosmetic Unlocks** — at specific milestone levels, new visual themes for platforms and characters are unlocked. These are purely cosmetic and do not affect gameplay.

## New Item Types in Higher Levels

As you progress past Level 7, the item roster expands. Here is a summary of what to expect:

| Item | First Appears | Location | Scoring Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turbo Soda | Level 1 | Upper-middle row | Standard |
| Turbo Chips | Level 1 | Lower-middle row | Standard |
| Power Soda | Level 7 | Any middle row | 2× standard |
| Mega Chips | Level 10 | Any row | 3× standard |
| Rare Drop | Level 13 | Random row | 5× standard |

## Difficulty Scaling Summary

Difficulty in Turbo scales across three primary axes as you advance through level tiers:

1. **Item density** — more items occupy more platform segments simultaneously, leaving fewer blank safe zones.
2. **Spawn frequency** — cleared items are replaced more quickly, reducing the window you have to move freely.
3. **Gap tolerance** — the margin for error when jumping the center gap decreases, requiring more precise timing and momentum control.

Staying aware of your current tier and preparing for the next milestone unlock gives you the strategic foundation to keep climbing through Turbo's progressively intense level screens.
