Turbo is a fast-paced arcade platformer that puts you on a series of stacked wooden platforms, challenging you to collect valuable items while dodging dangerous hazards before the clock runs out. Each level is built from horizontal rows of wooden planks split into left and right segments with a central gap — mastering the layout is the key to survival and high scores.

## What Is Turbo?

In Turbo, you navigate multiple vertical tiers of platforms arranged across the screen. Your goal is simple: collect as many **Turbo Chips** as you can while steering clear of **Turbo Soda**. Every chip you grab earns you points, and every soda you touch costs you. The deeper you push into each level, the faster the pace becomes and the higher the rewards — and risks — grow.

## Core Game Loop

The moment you drop onto the first platform, the loop begins:

1. **Navigate** — Move left and right across wooden platform segments, hopping between tiers through the central gap or platform edges.
2. **Collect** — Grab Turbo Chips (marked by a distinctive green glow) scattered across the platforms to earn points.
3. **Avoid** — Steer clear of Turbo Soda (marked by a red glow) to protect your score and keep your run alive.
4. **Progress** — Advance through increasingly demanding levels as the platform arrangements grow more complex.

<Note>
  The color-coded glow system is your most important visual cue. Green means reward; red means danger. When in doubt, follow the green glow.
</Note>

## Game Elements Overview

Understanding the building blocks of Turbo sets you up for success from your very first run.

| Element | Description | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Wooden Platforms | Horizontal wooden planks in left/right segments | Your traversal surface across each tier |
| Central Gap | The open space between left and right platform segments | Used to drop down or jump between tiers |
| Blank Bottom Platform | The lowest-tier row — two wooden plank segments with a center gap and no items on it | Safe landing zone and starting area; a reliable reset point when falling |
| Turbo Chips | Small collectibles marked by a green glow | Award points when collected |
| Turbo Soda | Hazard items marked by a red glow | Deduct points or end your run on contact |

Every element on screen has a purpose. As you play more rounds, reading the layout at a glance becomes second nature — and that's when your scores will really start to climb.
