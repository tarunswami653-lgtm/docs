Turbo Chips are the reward you're always hunting for on the wooden platforms of Turbo. Packaged in a vivid green bag labeled **TURBO Chips original**, these snacks are your ticket to a higher score, faster movement, and the kind of combo chains that push you up the leaderboard. Every green-glowing platform is an opportunity — and learning to prioritize Turbo Chips collection over everything else is the foundation of a strong run.

## What Turbo Chips Look Like

Turbo Chips appear as a green chip bag standing on a wooden platform segment. The bag is labeled **TURBO Chips original** in bold branding, rendered in a bright green color scheme that stands out against the earthy tones of the game's platforms. The bag's upright posture and consistent size make it easy to spot even in the middle of a fast-moving run.

Unlike the red-and-white danger aesthetic of Turbo Soda, the green bag immediately reads as positive — and that's intentional. Your eye is trained to associate green with "go," and Turbo rewards that instinct with real in-game benefits.

## The Green Glow Reward Signal

Beneath every Turbo Chips bag, the platform pulses with a **green glow**. This glow radiates outward rhythmically, just like the red glow under Turbo Soda — but the color tells you the opposite story. Green means reward, and it means you should be actively steering toward that platform.

<Tip>
The green glow is visible from much farther away than the item label itself. Train yourself to scan for green-glowing platforms first as you approach new sections — this lets you plan your collection route several steps ahead, especially in fast or obstacle-dense areas where close-range reading isn't reliable.
</Tip>

The glow pulses as long as the Turbo Chips bag is present. Once you collect it, the glow disappears and the platform returns to its neutral state. If you see a green glow on a platform you've already passed, you may have missed a pickup — factor that into your route on the next attempt.

## What You Gain by Collecting Turbo Chips

Picking up a Turbo Chips bag triggers one or more of the following positive effects:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Points Boost" icon="star">
    Each Turbo Chips collected adds a direct points bonus to your score. The base value increases the further you progress in a run.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Speed Boost" icon="bolt">
    Collecting Turbo Chips temporarily increases your movement speed, helping you reach more items and cover more ground before the effect wears off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Combo Multiplier" icon="layer-group">
    Consecutive Turbo Chips pickups without touching a hazard build a combo multiplier, amplifying the points value of each subsequent chip bag you collect.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

These effects can combine when you collect multiple bags in quick succession. A clean streak of Turbo Chips pickups — especially with the combo multiplier active — is the fastest way to push your score to competitive levels.

## Collection Bonuses and Effects

The benefit you get from each Turbo Chips bag scales with your current game state:

| Situation | Effect |
|---|---|
| First bag of the run | Base points bonus applied |
| Speed boost active | Duration of boost refreshed/extended |
| Combo chain of 2+ | Points multiplier increases with each pickup |
| After a Turbo Soda hit | Speed and combo reset; Turbo Chips restart your chain |
| Late-game (high distance) | Base points value is multiplied by a run-progress bonus |

<Note>
The combo multiplier resets if you touch a Turbo Soda item, fall off a platform, or miss too many collection windows in a row. Keeping your chain unbroken is the highest-value skill to develop as you improve.
</Note>

## Strategy for Prioritizing Collection

Turbo Chips are the engine of your score, so building your movement decisions around collecting them is always the right call. Use these strategies to maximize your pickup rate:

- **Route through green glows first.** When you see multiple platforms ahead, mentally draw a line through the green-glowing ones. That's your ideal path — adjust your movement to follow it.
- **Use the speed boost offensively.** When a speed boost is active after collecting Turbo Chips, use the extra velocity to reach the next green-glowing platform faster, potentially triggering another boost before the first one expires.
- **Protect your combo above all else.** A combo multiplier at 4× or 5× makes each Turbo Chips bag worth far more than its base value. Sacrifice a shortcut or an easier path to avoid breaking a combo chain.
- **Recover quickly after a miss.** If you bypass a Turbo Chips bag, don't dwell on it — find the next green glow and re-engage. A missed pickup is recoverable; a Turbo Soda hit while distracted is not.
- **Learn spawn patterns.** As you play more runs, you'll begin to notice tendencies in where Turbo Chips appear relative to platform clusters. Use that pattern knowledge to anticipate green glows before they come into full view.
