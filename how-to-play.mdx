Understanding the rules and controls behind Turbo transforms a confusing first run into a deliberate, high-scoring performance. This page covers everything you need to know — from how to move your character to what ends a run — so you can focus on building skill rather than guessing mechanics.

## Controls

Turbo supports both keyboard and gamepad input. All core actions are available on both setups, so use whichever feels most comfortable.

### Keyboard

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move Left | `←` Arrow / `A` |
| Move Right | `→` Arrow / `D` |
| Jump / Move Up Tier | `↑` Arrow / `W` / `Space` |
| Drop Down Tier | `↓` Arrow / `S` |
| Pause | `Escape` / `P` |

### Gamepad

| Action | Button |
|---|---|
| Move Left / Right | Left Stick or D-Pad Left/Right |
| Jump / Move Up Tier | `A` (Xbox) / `Cross` (PlayStation) |
| Drop Down Tier | Left Stick Down or D-Pad Down |
| Pause | `Start` / `Options` |

<Note>
  Controls can be remapped in **Settings → Controls** from the main menu. Custom bindings are saved automatically between sessions.
</Note>

## Core Rules

Turbo runs on a small set of rules that are easy to learn and take time to master:

- **Collect Turbo Chips** (green glow) to earn points. The more you collect in a single run, the higher your final score.
- **Avoid Turbo Soda** (red glow) at all times. Contact with a Turbo Soda item deducts points from your current score.
- **Stay on the platforms.** Falling off the edge of a platform or through the central gap without intending to drops you to a lower tier — or out of the level entirely.
- **Time matters.** Each level has a countdown. Running out of time ends your run regardless of score.
- **You cannot revisit collected items.** Once a Turbo Chip is collected, it disappears from the platform for the rest of that run.

## Platform Movement Rules

Platforms in Turbo follow consistent rules that you can rely on once you learn them:

- Each platform row is divided into a **left segment** and a **right segment**, separated by a **central gap**.
- You move horizontally by walking or running along a platform segment. You reach the segment's end when you hit the screen edge or the boundary of the central gap.
- To **move up a tier**, jump while standing near the central gap or at a platform edge where an upper tier overlaps. Your character will land on the platform above.
- To **move down a tier**, press the drop input while standing over the central gap or a platform edge. You'll fall through to the platform below.
- Platform segments do **not** wrap around the screen — you cannot walk off the left edge and reappear on the right.

<Warning>
  Falling through the **central gap** without pressing the drop input intentionally can send you further down than expected. If you're on a low tier and fall accidentally, you may drop out of the level and lose your current run. Always be deliberate when crossing or using the central gap.
</Warning>

## Item Interaction Rules

Every item on a platform behaves according to a fixed set of interactions:

- **Turbo Chips** — Move over them to collect automatically. No input required. Awards a fixed point value, with bonuses for chains collected in quick succession.
- **Turbo Soda** — Contact applies an immediate point penalty. There is no way to neutralize a Turbo Soda item; the only option is to avoid it.
- **Special Items** — Appear on higher tiers as you progress. Each has a unique icon and effect. Some grant temporary speed boosts; others freeze nearby hazards briefly. Read the in-game item glossary for a full list.
- **Item Respawning** — Turbo Chips and Turbo Soda do not respawn once collected or triggered. Plan your path to maximize chip collection before clearing a tier.

## Winning and Losing

**You win a run** by reaching the score target for the current level before the timer expires. Completing a level unlocks the next stage and adds a bonus to your total score.

**Your run ends** when any of the following occur:

- The level timer reaches zero before you hit the target score.
- You fall out of the level through an unintended gap drop with no remaining recovery items.
- Your score drops to zero as a result of repeated Turbo Soda contact (in modes where score-floor loss is enabled).

After a run ends, your final score is recorded on the leaderboard. Studying where your score stalled — too many Turbo Soda hits, missed chip chains, or poor tier transitions — is the fastest way to improve your next attempt.
