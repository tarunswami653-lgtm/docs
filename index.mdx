Welcome to the official Turbo documentation! Turbo is a fast-paced arcade platformer where you dash across wooden platform tiers, snagging glowing green Turbo Chips for big rewards while steering clear of red-glowing Turbo Soda cans that'll set you back. Whether you're a first-time player or a seasoned speedrunner, this guide has everything you need.
