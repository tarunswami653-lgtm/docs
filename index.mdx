Welcome to the official Turbo documentation! Turbo is a fast-paced arcade platformer where you dash across multi-tier wooden platforms, snagging glowing green Turbo Chips for big rewards while steering clear of red-glowing Turbo Soda cans that'll set you back. Each level is built from horizontal rows of wooden plank segments separated by a center gap — and at the very bottom sits the blank wooden platform, an empty lowest-tier row with no items, giving you a safe zone to land and regroup. Whether you're a first-time player or a seasoned speedrunner, this guide has everything you need.
