Whether you're just picking up Turbo for the first time or you've been playing for a while and hit a confusing moment, this FAQ covers the questions players ask most often. If you don't find what you're looking for here, check the relevant section of the docs or reach out to support.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the blank bottom wooden platform?">
    The blank bottom wooden platform is the **lowest row in every level screen** — it is a completely empty, floor-level platform with no items on it. No Turbo Soda or Turbo Chips appear on this row at the start of any level, making it your **safe zone and starting area**.

    When you begin a new level screen, you are placed on the bottom row. You can use it as a home base to survey the upper rows before committing to a move. Because it is blank, you won't accidentally trigger any item interactions while you're standing on it.

    Keep in mind that in higher difficulty tiers (Level 11 and beyond), the bottom row can begin filling with items mid-screen as dynamic spawning kicks in. Until that point, the blank bottom wooden platform is reliably the safest spot on screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does the red glow mean?">
    A red glow on a platform segment or item is a **warning indicator**. It signals one of the following conditions:

    - **Danger zone** — the item or platform is in a state that will cause damage or a penalty if you interact with it incorrectly.
    - **Hazard item** — some higher-level item types pulse red to indicate they must be avoided rather than collected.
    - **Countdown warning** — on timed screens, a red glow can indicate that the time limit for a particular item is nearly expired.

    When you see red, slow down, reassess your position, and make sure you understand what the glow is attached to before you move. Rushing into a red-glowing item is one of the most common ways to lose a streak.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does the green glow mean?">
    A green glow is a **positive indicator** — it means something beneficial is available or active. Common causes of a green glow include:

    - **Collectible item ready** — the item is primed and will award bonus points or a power-up when collected.
    - **Combo window active** — your combo multiplier is live and the next item you collect will extend your streak.
    - **Safe platform confirmed** — in certain level types, green-glowing platforms are explicitly marked as safe to land on, even if their appearance suggests otherwise.

    When you see green, prioritize moving toward it. A green-glowing item collected during an active combo multiplier produces the highest possible score values.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I avoid falling through the center gap?">
    The center gap is the open space between the LEFT and RIGHT platform segments on every row. Falling through it drops you to the row below, and from the bottom row, a gap fall has the most severe consequences.

    Here are the key techniques for avoiding accidental gap falls:

    - **Watch your momentum** — your character's horizontal speed determines whether a jump clears the gap or falls short. Practice the jump arc from both the left and right segments so you can time it consistently.
    - **Don't rush across rows** — moving quickly up or down between rows near the center of the screen puts you at the highest risk of drifting into the gap. Move vertically near the outer edges of the platform segments when possible.
    - **Use the bottom row as a reset** — if you find yourself in a dangerous position near the gap on an upper row, it is often safer to drop deliberately to the bottom row and reposition than to attempt a risky crossing.
    - **Learn the gap width by level tier** — the gap width can vary slightly between level tiers. Spend your first run on a new tier's screen getting a feel for the gap before committing to fast play.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is my score calculated?">
    Your score in Turbo is calculated from several contributing factors that compound throughout your run:

    | Factor | How It Adds to Your Score |
    |---|---|
    | Base item collection | Each Turbo Soda or Turbo Chips collected adds a flat point value |
    | Combo multiplier | Collecting items in rapid succession applies a multiplier (activates at Level 4+) |
    | Speed bonus | Clearing a screen faster than par time adds a time-based bonus |
    | Perfect clear bonus | Clearing all items without a gap fall adds a multiplier for the next screen |
    | Higher-tier items | Power Soda, Mega Chips, and Rare Drops have 2×, 3×, and 5× base values respectively |
    | Streak reward | Consecutive clean screens add compounding streak bonuses |

    Your cumulative score also determines when you unlock new level tiers, so maintaining consistent collection habits — even without a perfect run — matters more than chasing single high-value items at the cost of your streak.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I unlock new levels?">
    New level screens unlock when you meet one or both of the following conditions on your current screen:

    1. **Clear the required items** — collect or neutralize all active items (Turbo Soda, Turbo Chips, and any higher-tier variants) on the current screen. Once the required items are gone, the screen transition triggers automatically.
    2. **Reach the score threshold** — some levels require a cumulative score minimum before the next screen unlocks. Early tiers (Levels 1–3) only require item clearing, but from Level 4 onward, score gates become an additional requirement.

    If you've cleared a screen but the transition hasn't triggered, check whether your cumulative score has hit the threshold for your current tier. Refer to the [Unlocks and Progression](/levels/unlocks) page for a full breakdown of score thresholds by level tier.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
