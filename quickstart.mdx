Getting into Turbo is quick — within a few minutes you'll be on your feet, navigating wooden platforms, scooping up glowing collectibles, and learning to spot danger before it hits. This guide walks you through everything from installation to your first successful run.

<Tip>
  Turbo uses a **color-coded glow system** to signal intent at a glance. Items with a **green glow** are Turbo Chips — grab them for points. Items with a **red glow** are Turbo Soda — avoid them at all costs. Training your eye on these two colors before anything else is the single fastest way to improve.
</Tip>

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install or Download Turbo">
    Head to the official Turbo distribution page or your platform's game store and download the latest version of Turbo. Once the download completes, run the installer and follow the on-screen prompts. The installation is lightweight — no external dependencies are required to get the game running.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch the Game and Reach the Main Menu">
    Open Turbo from your desktop shortcut or application folder. You'll land on the main menu, where you can start a new game, review the leaderboard, or adjust your settings. Before jumping in, take a moment to visit **Settings** and confirm your display and control preferences match your setup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Understand the Platform Layout">
    When a level loads, you'll see a series of **horizontal platform rows** stacked vertically on screen. Each row is made up of two wooden plank segments — one on the left side of the screen and one on the right — with a **central gap** running down the middle. You can traverse left and right along each row and use the central gap or platform edges to move between tiers. Spend your first run just getting comfortable with this structure before chasing items.

    <Note>
      Platform rows are arranged from bottom to top. The **blank bottom wooden platform** is the lowest-tier row — it has no items on it and acts as a safe landing zone. It's the ideal place to get your bearings if you fall from a higher tier. Once you're ready, work your way upward where Turbo Chips and hazards appear.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect Your First Turbo Chips">
    Once you're in a level, look for items emitting a **green glow** resting on the platform planks. Move your character over a Turbo Chip to collect it automatically — no button press required. Each chip you collect adds to your score, shown in the top corner of the screen. Try to plan a path that lets you chain multiple chips in one smooth run across a tier.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Avoid Turbo Soda">
    As you collect chips, watch out for items glowing **red** — these are Turbo Soda, and touching one will penalize your score. Turbo Soda appears in the same spots as Turbo Chips, so you'll need to read the glow colors quickly as you move. If a red-glow item is blocking your ideal path, choose a longer route rather than risking the penalty. With practice, dodging becomes instinctive.
  </Step>
</Steps>
